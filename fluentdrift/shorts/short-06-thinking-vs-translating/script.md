# Short #6 - Stop translating. Start picturing. (Diego's story)

**Duration:** 38 seconds
**Voice:** ElevenLabs Brian, 155 wpm, warm storyteller
**Re-watch hooks:** Loop trick (handwritten letter unfurls in opening,
folds back closing), hidden detail (date stamp on Diego's email reads
"Day 14" - the exact day his brain switched), punchline reveal (open is
'his brain was loud', close is 'his brain went quiet'), info density (6
years vs 14 days, 3 drills, 1 result)

---

## Spoken lines

[0:00 - 0:03] HOOK
> Diego translated every English sentence for six years.

[0:03 - 0:07] PAIN
> Apps. Tutors. Tests. Meditation. Nothing stopped his brain from
> doing it.

[0:07 - 0:10] PIVOT
> Then he tried something different.

[0:10 - 0:14] FRAME
> Three minutes a day. Three simple drills.

[0:14 - 0:21] PROTOCOL
> Name every object he saw. Narrate every action he did. Picture
> every sentence he heard.

[0:21 - 0:25] BUILD
> Total time. Forty-two minutes. Spread over fourteen days.

[0:25 - 0:30] PAYOFF
> On day fifteen, he had his first conversation in English without
> translating a single word.

[0:30 - 0:34] PUNCHLINE
> He said it felt like the volume of his mind turned down.

[0:34 - 0:38] LOOP
> Six years. Forty-two minutes. The difference was the method.
