# Short #5 - 3 steps to stop translating in your head

**Duration:** 35 seconds
**Voice:** ElevenLabs Brian, 165 wpm
**Re-watch hooks:** Loop trick (3-icon row matches at start and end),
hidden detail (timer in corner counts up to 03:00 over the duration -
the actual drill time), punchline reveal (opens "your brain is
broken", closes "your brain works perfectly"), info density (3 drills
+ frequency + duration + result in 35s)

---

## Spoken lines

[0:00 - 0:02] HOOK
> Your English brain is broken. Three minutes a day fixes it.

[0:02 - 0:05] FRAME
> Three drills. One minute each. Fourteen days.

[0:05 - 0:11] DRILL 1
> Step one. Object Naming. Walk through a room. Name every object out
> loud. In English. Just the noun.

[0:11 - 0:17] DRILL 2
> Step two. Verb Mapping. Walking, cooking, brushing. Narrate the
> action in present tense. Out loud. In English.

[0:17 - 0:25] DRILL 3
> Step three. Image Replacement. When someone speaks to you, do not
> translate. Picture what they said.

[0:25 - 0:30] PAYOFF
> Three minutes a day. Fourteen days. Translation habit dies.

[0:30 - 0:33] PUNCHLINE
> Your brain isn't broken. It just needs new wiring.

[0:33 - 0:35] LOOP
> Three minutes. Fourteen days.
