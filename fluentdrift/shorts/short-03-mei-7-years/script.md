## Cadence notes

- Opening line ("Mei was stuck") - low register, warm, almost
  whispered
- "Books. Apps. Tutors. Tests." - each word = its own beat,
  staccato
- "Then she stopped." - full stop, 0.6s pause before next line
- Drills list - even pacing, no excitement
- "Twenty-two minutes a day. Forty-two days total." - emphasized
  numbers via SSML
- "She just passed" - voice lifts 10% in pitch (small joy)
- Final lines - return to warm storyteller register
- Last word "wasn't" - trail off, leaves space for the loop
