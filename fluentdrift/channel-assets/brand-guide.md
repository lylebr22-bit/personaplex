# FluentDrift - Brand Guide

## Channel identity

- **Name:** FluentDrift
- **Tagline:** *Think in English. Drift into fluency.*
- **Handle:** `@fluentdrift` (lock on YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Instagram)
- **Audience:** Intermediate English learners (CEFR B1-B2), 18-45,
  globally distributed with strongest concentration in India, Brazil,
  Vietnam, Egypt, Mexico

## Voice + tone

| Dimension      | Direction                                            |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Register       | Warm authority - trusted mentor, not news anchor     |
| Formality      | Conversational, contractions allowed except in drill instructions |
| Speed          | 140-150 wpm narration (slower than native pace)      |
| Humor          | Dry, rare, never at the viewer's expense             |
| Pronouns       | "You" (intimate), "we" (communal), never "they"      |

## Visual system

### Color palette

| Role         | Hex      | Usage                                |
|--------------|----------|--------------------------------------|
| Navy base    | #0B1220  | Backgrounds, authority tone          |
| Near-black   | #000000  | Gradient bottom, thumbnail canvas    |
| Gold signal  | #F5C84C  | Breakthrough, key emphasis           |
| Red alert    | #E63946  | Pattern interrupts, crossed-out errors|
| Cream paper  | #F5EFE0  | Story beats (Mei's email, letters)   |
| Pure white   | #FFFFFF  | Primary text on dark backgrounds     |

### Typography

| Role                 | Font                          | Where                         |
|----------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Thumbnail display    | Anton or Bebas Neue Bold      | 3-word punch overlays         |
| Sub-headers / labels | Inter Bold                    | On-screen text, chapter titles|
| Numerals / timers    | JetBrains Mono                | Countdowns, data callouts     |

## Production cadence

- **Release rhythm:** 5 episodes/week, Mon-Fri, 07:00 UTC publish time
- **Episode length:** 7-9 minutes long-form + 3 Shorts cut from each
- **Sponsor slot:** Max 1 per episode, mid-roll at the 4:30-5:00 mark

## Non-negotiables

1. Faceless always - silhouettes, hands, environments only
2. Captions hardcoded on every video (ESL audience parses text + audio)
3. One affiliate link per video maximum
4. Every episode ships with 3 vertical Shorts to maximize top-of-funnel
5. Community tab poll within 48h of every long-form upload
