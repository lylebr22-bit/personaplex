# Storyboard - Episode 2

15 scenes x ~30 seconds = 7:30 runtime.

This storyboard is in compact form (single line per scene) since
the visual grammar is established by Episode 1. Editor should
follow Episode 1's storyboard.md for shot vocabulary, LUT choices,
sticky-bar style, and pattern-interrupt aesthetics.

## Asset re-use from Episode 1

These Episode 1 assets carry over with minor recolor:
- `S04-brains-split` -> Scene 5 (different color labels)
- `S06-interrupt-timer` -> Scene 6 (5s instead of 30s)
- `S09-interrupt-library` -> Scene 9 (Stanford instead of Cambridge - swap watermark)
- `S13-mei-story` template -> Scene 13 (Diego instead of Mei)
- `S14-synthesis-cards` -> Scene 14 (1+1+1 instead of 2+10+10)

Reusing 6 of 15 scene templates cuts production time roughly in half.
