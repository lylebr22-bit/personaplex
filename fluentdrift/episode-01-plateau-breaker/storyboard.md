# Storyboard - Episode 1

16 scenes x 30 seconds = 8:00 runtime.
Each scene maps 1:1 to `script.md`. Asset IDs reference
`assets.json`, which editors can load into their project manager.

## Editor notes

1. **Hardcode captions** across all 16 scenes - ESL audience requires it
2. **No scene holds >4 seconds** without a motion or text change
3. **Apply interrupt-specific LUTs** to Scenes 6, 9, 13 - the color
   shift signals "something different is happening" to the viewer
4. **Sticky bars for drills** should re-appear in Scenes 14 and 15
   for free repetition
5. **End card starts at 7:45** - gives the next-video thumbnail a full
   15 seconds of click zone
