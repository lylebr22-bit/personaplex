## Pinned comments (rotate)

### Pin #1 - Participation challenge (pin at upload)

```
Your homework before you keep watching

Stop reading this. Look around the room. Describe 3 objects out
loud in English - right now. Full sentences. 30 seconds.

Then come back and drop your score in the comments:

GREEN - spoke fluently, no stumbling
YELLOW - stumbled a few times, but did it
RED - froze or switched to my native language

No judgement. This is exactly the muscle we're training.

Honest replies only - 80% of you are about to write RED and
that's the whole point of this episode.
```

### Pin #2 - Level confession (pin at T+24h)

```
A quick question for everyone - please be honest, no one is
judging:

How many years have you been "intermediate" in English?

- 0-2 years
- 3-5 years
- 6-10 years
- 10+ years (this was me before I figured out the 3 drills)

I'm reading every reply for the first 48 hours and designing
next week's episode around the most common answer. Tell me below
- and I'll pin my favorite story to the top of the video.

Your answer might become the hook for the next episode.
```

### Pin #3 - Accountability thread (pin at T+72h)


Comment-to-view ratio **>= 1.5%** in the first 48 hours. Below
that, the hook or title is the bottleneck.
