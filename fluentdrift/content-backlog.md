## Topic generation rules

When the backlog runs low, generate new topics by combining:

- **Pain** (freeze, translate, mispronounce, forget, sound formal)
- **Specificity** (a CEFR level, a country, a job, a scene)
- **Promise** (in 30s, in 7 days, in one drill)
- **Authority** (a study, a name, a number)

Example combinations:
- Pain (freeze) + Specificity (Zoom calls) + Promise (4 phrases) -> "4 phrases that unfreeze your English on Zoom"
- Pain (translate) + Specificity (Spanish speakers) + Promise (3 days) -> "3 days to stop translating from Spanish to English"

## Quality gate

Every topic in the backlog must pass:

1. Has it been searched 1K+ times/month? (use TubeBuddy or vidIQ)
2. Is the answer concrete and demonstrable in <45s?
3. Could you build the visual mid-flight without filming?
4. Does it have a clear loop trick?

If 4/4 yes, it's in the backlog. If <4/4, rewrite the topic.
