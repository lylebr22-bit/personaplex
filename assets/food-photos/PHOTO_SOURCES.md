## Download Tips

- **Unsplash**: Click the download button on any photo page for full resolution (often 4K+)
- **Pexels**: Click "Free Download" and select "Original" size for highest resolution
- **Pixabay**: Click download and select the largest resolution available
- **All sites above**: Free for commercial use, no attribution required (though credit is appreciated)

## Quick Download Script

Use the included `download_photos.sh` script to batch download recommended photos:
```bash
cd assets/food-photos
chmod +x download_photos.sh
./download_photos.sh
```
